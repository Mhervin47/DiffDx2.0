HOW IT WORKS

A structured approach to better diagnosis

DiffDx uses a three-loop architecture to conduct a clinical interview, evaluate each turn, and determine the most likely conditions.

1
Loop 1 · Actor

Clinical Interview (Actor)

Asks adaptive, follow-up questions based on your responses.

2
Loop 2 · Critic

Evaluation (Critic)

Scores each turn for accuracy, safety, and completeness.

3
Loop 3 · Router

Triage & Guidance (Router)

Identifies urgency, suggests relevant specialties, and flags ambiguity.
